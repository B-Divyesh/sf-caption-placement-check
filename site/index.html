How caption checking works

Check placement before publishing

Caption tools make words. This check keeps those words from hiding the image.

  1. 01

    Pair the final exports

    Drop in the video and its SRT or WebVTT file. The checker processes them locally.

  2. 02

    Check each caption

    The checker samples each caption and looks for faces or visually busy areas.

  3. 03

    Review and export alerts

    Check each time, compare a safer position, mark protected regions, and export the alerts.
