The preflight bench

One focused pass before release

Caption generators solve the words. This check helps protect the image those words sit on.

  1. 01

    Pair the final exports

    Drop in the video and its SRT or WebVTT file. Files stay in local memory.

  2. 02

    Sample every cue

    The checker seeks into each timed cue and looks for face or dense visual overlap.

  3. 03

    Make the call

    Review exact timecodes, compare a safer position, mark protected regions, and export the list.
